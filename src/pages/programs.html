Questions

Good to know.

Do I pay anything to apply?

No. Applying is free and commits you to nothing. Founding membership is by application and interview, and payment is only requested once a place is confirmed.

Can I put my Berlin Lab ticket toward the Mastermind?

Yes. If you enroll within seven days of the Berlin Lab on 22 October, your full €795 is credited toward your membership.

What's included in a membership, and what isn't?

Your membership agreement sets out the advisory time, sessions, immersion days, and confidentiality terms included. Corporate system licenses, custom integrations, and ongoing deployment costs are not included. Real AI Dynamics can scope them separately.

Can I work on confidential company problems?

Yes, within limits. Exercises and demonstrations use approved or sanitized information, which matters especially in healthcare and pharma. Smart-glasses demonstrations only happen with explicit consent, and no-recording zones apply.

Where are the immersions held?

The program is global, with rotating host cities. Founding members hear the inaugural location and dates first.
